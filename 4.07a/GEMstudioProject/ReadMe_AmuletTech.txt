The AmuletTech directory structure mimics that of the C:\ProgramData\AmuletTech folder to demonstrate where the custom files should be placed on your own machine, due to the custom Touchpanel and LCD used for this project. The files that do not come standard on regular GEMstudio installations which are required by this project are:

AmuletTech\Global\Configuration\Init\Init Touchpanel FlipY.ini
AmuletTech\Global\Configuration\LCD\800 x 600\AMP\AM-800600C1-TMQW-T01H.tft
